Website Setup & Admin Guide

Your package has 3 files: index.html (the public website), admin.html (staff dashboard) and Code.gs (the Google Sheets backend).

Thailand-only now. Philippines destinations, packages and offers have been removed from the site and the dashboard, which now focuses on Bangkok, Phuket, Pattaya, Krabi, Chiang Mai and Ayutthaya. If your Sheet's Images/Prices tabs still have old rows for Philippines destinations (e.g. boracay, palawan), they're harmless and ignored by the site — delete those rows in the Sheet if you'd like to tidy up.
New architecture (tour-operator style). The homepage now runs: a rotating promo carousel (hero) → About → a "One Stop Service" tile grid → a horizontal "Popular Tours" row with HOT badges and optional strikethrough pricing → the trip planner → Destinations → Offers → the rest as before. There's also a new All Tours page (#/tours, in the header's "Travel Services" menu) with search, filter pills (Tour Type, Duration, Vehicle, Tour Style) and a price slider.

Part 1: First-time setup

Step 1. Create the database

  1. Create a new Google Sheet. Name it anything, e.g. “Travel Leads”.
  2. Click Extensions → Apps Script.
  3. Delete the sample code, paste all of Code.gs.
  4. Edit the top lines: ADMIN_EMAIL (you), NOTIFY_EMAIL (who gets new-lead alerts), AGENCY (your business name), and ADMIN_URL (leave as a placeholder for now — come back and set it once your dashboard is online in Step 5, then redeploy).
  5. Click Save.
Keep the Sheet private. Do not share this Google Sheet with staff. Only the owner's Google account should have access to it. Staff work through admin.html only, which talks to the Sheet through this script.

Step 2. Run setup once

  1. In the function dropdown at the top, choose setup, then click Run.
  2. Approve the permissions (Sheets, Gmail, Drive). If Google shows “unverified app”, choose Advanced → Continue. It is your own script.
  3. Three tabs appear in the Sheet: Leads, Users, Images. A temporary admin password is emailed to ADMIN_EMAIL.

Step 3. Publish the backend

  1. Click Deploy → New deployment, gear icon → Web app.
  2. Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone. Click Deploy.
  3. Copy the Web app URL (ends with /exec).

Step 4. Put the URL in the website files

Open index.html in a text editor (Notepad or similar). Near the top find the SETTINGS block:

window.APP_CONFIG={
  API_URL:"PASTE_WEB_APP_URL",
  WHATSAPP:"66000000000"
};

Replace PASTE_WEB_APP_URL with your URL (keep the quotes) and set your WhatsApp number (country code, no + or spaces). Do the same in admin.html, or leave admin as is and paste the URL in the box on its sign-in screen.

Step 5. Put the site online

  1. Put index.html and admin.html in one folder.
  2. Netlify: go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag the folder in. Cloudflare Pages: Create project → Upload assets.
  3. Your site is live at yoursite.netlify.app. Add your own domain under Domain settings and paste the DNS records at your domain registrar.
  4. Your dashboard is at yoursite/admin.html.

Step 6. Test

Submit a test quote on the site. A row should appear in the Leads tab, the sales email should arrive, and the Lead ID should show on the thank-you screen.

Part 2: The dashboard layout

admin.html now has a left-hand navigation (a bar across the top on phones) with: Leads, Quotes, Content & Images, Users, Account, Guide. Agents see Leads, Quotes, Account and Guide. Only Admins see Content & Images and Users.

Part 3: Changing pictures, prices and text (Content & Images tab)

Where: sign in as an Admin → Content & Images.

Agency & contact details

Edit agency name, phone, WhatsApp number, LINE ID, email, hours and office address, then Save settings. These appear across the site (header, footer, contact section, WhatsApp and call buttons) within a few minutes.

Photos

  1. Find the slot: Home hero banner or a destination (Bangkok, Phuket, … Siargao).
  2. Click Choose file and pick a photo. It is resized and uploaded to Google Drive automatically.
  3. It appears on the site within about 5 minutes. Hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R) if you still see the old one.
  4. Remove photo returns the slot to the coloured gradient.
Photo tips: landscape, about 1600×900, under 1.5 MB. Keep the subject away from the bottom-left (text sits there). Only use photos you own or are licensed to use.

Starting prices

Next to each destination (except the hero), type the "From" price shown on its card and page, e.g. ฿ 12,900, then Save price.

Tour packages, special offers, testimonials

Each has its own card list with + Add, editable fields, an Active checkbox (unticking hides it from the site without deleting it), and Remove. Click the section's Save button once you're done editing that section — it replaces the whole list on the site.

Each package also has: Mark HOT (shows a red HOT badge), an optional Was price (shown with a strikethrough next to the regular price, for a discount look), a numeric Price in THB (used by the All Tours page's price slider — separate from the display price text, which can say anything like "฿ 1,200/Pax"), and four tags — Tour Type, Duration, Vehicle, Tour Style — used by the All Tours filters. Leaving a tag blank ("—") just means that tour won't match a filter for that category.

Package Inclusions are entered one per line. Everything you don't edit here (FAQ, page layout, "Why choose us", the "One Stop Service" tiles) still needs a direct edit to index.html, covered in Part 6.

A package or offer you add only appears on the site after you press that section's own Save button — adding a row just adds it to your screen first, so you can fill in several before saving.

About text

The paragraph under the hero on the homepage is set in Content & Images → Agency & contact details → About text.

Not appearing? Wait 5 minutes, hard refresh, and confirm API_URL is set correctly in index.html.

Part 4: Quotations (Quotes tab)

  1. Open a lead in Leads and click Quote this lead — or go straight to Quotes and fill in the customer's details.
  2. Add line items (hotel, transfers, tours…) with quantity and unit price. The total updates automatically.
  3. Set Valid until and any notes.
  4. Save quote keeps it as a draft. Save & mark lead QUOTE SENT also updates the linked lead's status and sets a follow-up date two days out.
  5. Print / Save PDF opens a clean printable quote in a new tab — use your browser's Print dialog and choose "Save as PDF" to send it to the customer.
  6. Reopen any quote later from the Saved quotes list to edit or reprint it.

Quote numbers look like QT-2026-000125 and are stored in the Sheet's Quotes tab. The printed quote shows your agency name at the top — set it once under Content & Images → Agency & contact details.

Part 5: Other admin tasks

Handle leads

  1. Sign in → Leads. Newest first. Filter by status if needed.
  2. Set status (NEW → CONTACTED → QUALIFIED → QUOTE SENT → NEGOTIATING → BOOKED; also LOST or FOLLOW-UP), assigned agent, next follow-up date and notes.
  3. Click Save on that lead.

Each lead also shows its source and UTM campaign. Use ad links like yoursite.com/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=thailand_october.

Add a staff member

  1. Users tab (Admins only) → enter name, email and role.
  2. Agent = Leads, Quotes, Account, Guide. Admin = everything, including Content & Images and Users.
  3. Click Create user. A temporary password is shown once; send it to them privately. They must change it at first sign-in.

Reset a password / deactivate a user

Users tab → Reset password or Deactivate (blocks sign-in; Activate restores it). You can't deactivate yourself.

Change your own password

Account tab → current password, new password (10+ characters with letters and numbers).

Forgot password (owner or any admin/agent)

On the sign-in screen, click Forgot your password?, enter the account's email, and click Send reset link. If that email has an account, a link arrives that opens a Set a new password screen — no old password needed. The link expires after 30 minutes.

For this to work, set ADMIN_URL near the top of Code.gs to your deployed admin.html address, e.g. https://yoursite.netlify.app/admin.html, then redeploy.

Owner locked out and can't receive email?

Open the Sheet → Extensions → Apps Script, choose resetOwnerPassword, Run. A new temporary password is emailed to ADMIN_EMAIL. Use this only as a backup to the reset-link option above.

Wrong password too many times

After 5 failures an account is locked for 15 minutes.

Who can see the Google Sheet

Only the owner. Everyone else — including admins on the dashboard — should never be given direct access to the Sheet. The dashboard already gives admins everything they need (leads, quotes, content, users) without touching the Sheet.

Part 6: Editing what's not in the dashboard yet

Agency name, contact details, prices, packages, offers and testimonials are now editable in Content & Images (Part 3). A few things still require a direct edit to index.html in a text editor, saved and re-uploaded to your host. Use Ctrl+F to find the text.

Privacy Policy / Terms linksPrivacy Policy in the footer — currently placeholders, add your real pages

Change alert emails or lead statuses

Edit the top of Code.gs in Apps Script. Then Deploy → Manage deployments → pencil icon → Version: New version → Deploy. Changes to Code.gs do nothing until you redeploy (the URL stays the same).

Analytics and ad pixels

The site sends events (lead_form_submitted, quote_requested, package_inquiry, whatsapp_clicked, phone_clicked) to a dataLayer. Paste your Google Analytics / Tag Manager / Meta Pixel snippet into the <head> of index.html to start receiving them.

Backups

Your Sheet is the database. Use File → Make a copy monthly, or File → Version history.

Security checklist

  • Protect /admin.html with Cloudflare Access or Netlify password protection, in addition to the sign-in.
  • Share the Google Sheet only with the owner. Staff use the dashboard.
  • Give staff the Agent role unless they need images or user management.
  • Deactivate staff accounts when people leave.
  • Publish a real Privacy Policy and Terms page before running ads.
